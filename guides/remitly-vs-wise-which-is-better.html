Remitly vs Wise: which is better?

Data updated on

It depends on the country, the amount and how your family will receive the money. Here are today's estimates for a $300 transfer from the United States, country by country.

How they are different

  • Wise uses the mid-market exchange rate and charges its fee separately, in plain view. It deposits the money into a bank account. It does not offer cash pickup.
  • Remitly earns part of its money in the exchange rate and part in the fee. In many countries your family can receive in a bank account or pick up cash.
  • Remitly often has promotions for new customers. They apply to the first transfer only, so check what you would get on the second one.

So which one should I use?

  • If your family needs to pick up cash, Wise will not work for you. Compare Remitly with the other services that offer cash pickup.
  • If your family has a bank account, use the one that delivers more on the day you send. Sometimes one wins and sometimes the other, and often a third service delivers more than both.

Remember: these are estimates, not guaranteed quotes. You see the final price in the app before you confirm.
