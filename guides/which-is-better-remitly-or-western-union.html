Which is better, Remitly or Western Union?

Data updated on

Neither one wins everywhere. It depends on the country you send to, the amount and the day. The fair way to compare is to look at how much your family actually receives. Here are today's estimates for a $300 transfer from the United States, country by country.

Is Remitly cheaper than Western Union?

Sometimes. Both services charge you in two ways: a fee you can see, and an exchange rate that is a little lower than the real market rate. A low fee does not mean more money arrives. The table above already includes both costs, so the bigger number is the better deal for that country today.

How they are alike

  • Both let you send money from the United States with an app or a website.
  • In many countries, your family can receive the money in a bank account or pick it up in cash.
  • Both often give new customers a special rate on the first transfer. Check what you would get on the second one.

How they are different

  • Western Union has many agent locations, so you can also send in person and pay in cash. Note: since 2026, paying for a transfer in cash in the United States carries a 1% federal tax. Paying from a bank account or with a card does not.
  • Remitly works mainly through its app and website.
  • With both, the price changes with how you pay (bank account, debit card, credit card) and how your family receives the money. Paying by card is usually faster and usually costs more.

So which one should I use?

Use the one that delivers more on the day you send, for your country and your amount. Sometimes Remitly wins, sometimes Western Union does, and often a third service delivers more than both. That is why it helps to see all the options side by side.

Remember: these are estimates, not guaranteed quotes. You see the final price in the app before you confirm.
